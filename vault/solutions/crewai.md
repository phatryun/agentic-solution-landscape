# CrewAI

CrewAI enables building multi-agent systems where each agent has a specific role,
goal, and backstory — they collaborate to accomplish complex tasks.

## Use cases
- Research automation
- Content pipelines
- Code generation teams
- Business process automation

## Strengths
- Intuitive role-based design
- Easy to get started
- Strong community growth
